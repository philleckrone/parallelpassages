Parallel Passages Editor is a Paratext tool that hides books, chapters or passages from Paratext's Parallel Passages tool, for example 1 and 2 Chronicles, so their many parallels with Samuel and Kings are not listed. You can also add parallel passages of your own. It edits Paratext's own ParallelPassages.xml, keeps the original file, and saves your lists so they can be put back after a Paratext update. Windows asks for administrator permission when it opens, because the file is in the Program Files folder. The changes apply to every project on the computer; restart Paratext after saving.

Download Install_Phils_tools.exe from the Paratext Plugins folder, and tick Parallel Passages Editor in it:
